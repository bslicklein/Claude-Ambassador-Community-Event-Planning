Take this with you

Six questions to ask before your next prompt

1
Have I done this before?
If yes, you are not writing a prompt. You are writing a skill.
2
Am I pasting context in by hand?
Every paste is a missing connector.
3
Does this only happen when I remember it?
Then it belongs on a schedule.
4
Should this start when something happens, not when I ask?
That is a managed agent.
5
Who checks the output, me or the system?
If the answer is you, you are missing a loop.
6
Is "good" written down anywhere?
If not, you have no bar to raise. Start with the evaluation.
