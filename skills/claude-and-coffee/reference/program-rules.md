# Anthropic ambassador program rules

These are program constraints, not preferences. Breaking one costs money or
credibility, and every line below came from a real event.

## The title

**Claude Community Ambassador for New York City.** Never "Claude Community
Leader". That title is wrong and it has shipped in published copy before. Check
every Luma description, LinkedIn post, and bio slide against this.

## The Luma page is not yours to publish

Submit the event request and **Anthropic builds the page**. That makes it a
dependency on somebody else's queue, which is why it sits at T-42 alongside
picking the date, ahead of everything else. Do not plan on a same-week page.

## Format is a money decision

| Format | What it carries |
|---|---|
| Meetup | Social, drop-in, no attendee credits |
| Workshop | **$50 of Claude credits per attendee**, structured teaching |
| Conversation | Panel or fireside, smaller |
| Impact Lab | $5,000 reimbursement tier instead of the normal caps |

Choosing Meetup for something that is really a Workshop leaves attendee credits
on the table, and you cannot reclassify it afterwards. Ask what people will
actually be doing for the middle ninety minutes, then pick.

## Do not promote speakers before the event

The program rule holds public speaker promotion until **after** the event. It
exists to protect people from last-minute schedule changes, and it applies to
LinkedIn, X, and the Luma page. Credit them generously in the recap instead.

## One event per week, per city

Two events forty-eight hours apart compete for the same audience and the same
energy, and neither gets full attention. When the calendar collides, **postpone
the weaker one rather than run a thin room**. A half-full event reads worse to
the people who did come than a rescheduled one does to the people who did not.

## Verticals

The NYC focus verticals are **fintech, legal, private equity, and education**.
Events outside them are fine and sometimes excellent, but be conscious that they
do not advance the positioning. When a quarter has served only one vertical,
that is the gap to fill next, not the topic that is easiest to book.
