# Money: caps, categories, and the invoice that has to exist first

The single most expensive lesson from Claude & Coffee NYC on 2026-08-11: total
spend $2,704.66, of which roughly **$1,110 was unrecoverable**, because the spend
went over the cap with no written pre-approval. None of that was a pricing
mistake. It was a paperwork sequence mistake.

## Tier caps, Tier A (New York)

| Attendees | Cap |
|---|---|
| 20–40 | $750 |
| 40–70 | $1,125 |
| 70+ | $1,500 |
| Impact Lab | $5,000 |

The cap covers **exactly three categories: venue, refreshments, printed assets.**
Staffing, easels, ticket rolls, and supplies are not covered, so keep them off the
claim entirely rather than trying to argue them in.

## The three rules that actually stall payments

1. **Itemized, never a lump sum.** Ask every venue for space rental separated from
   food and beverage *before booking*, not after the event. A venue that has
   already been paid has no reason to re-cut its invoice for you.
2. **An invoice link, not a payment or checkout link.** Approved platforms are
   Stripe, PayPal, Mercury, and Revolut.
3. **Pre-approval must come before the spend.** Over-cap needs written approval
   from community@anthropic.com *first*. Asking afterwards does not work.

When actual spend exceeds the cap anyway, the approved move is to **issue your own
itemized invoice for the amount being claimed**, not to submit the vendor's larger
invoice and ask for less.

## Submitting

Typeform `https://form.typeform.com/to/gam4NrSb`, **access code 419736**. The
ambassador submits it personally; vendors cannot submit on your behalf. Up to
three invoices per event.

**Payouts process every Friday.** Submitting on or after Friday rolls to the next
week, which is why the reimbursement gate sits at T+3 rather than "soon".

Do not send receipts to a program contact and do not use the old Google Form; it
is unmonitored.

## Deposits

A refundable security deposit is **not a claimable cost**. Chase it back from the
venue separately, and put a reminder on it, because nobody at the venue will.

## What a real Claude & Coffee costs

From the 2026-08-11 event, 100 attendees, as a planning baseline:

| Line | Actual |
|---|---|
| Venue rental | $750 |
| Staffing | $60 (not covered) |
| Food, 100 buns + quiche | $600 |
| Drink tab, ~100 drinks at menu price | $838 |
| Printed assets (banner, lanyards, name tags, stickers) | $349 |
| Welcome board, foam mounted | $73 |
| Easels, ticket rolls | $34 (not covered) |
| **Total** | **$2,705** |

The drink tab is the line that breaks the budget, and it is the one you find out
about at close. If the cap matters, agree a drink cap or a ticket count with the
venue up front and print the tickets.
